07 / AI & LLM QUALITY

Evaluating AI
beyond the answer.

Hands-on evaluation of LLM-generated responses used to support namescreening hit analysis, with emphasis on evidence, accuracy, and reliability.

Used Perplexity AI and other LLM tools to review namescreening hit assessments and compare generated explanations with underlying name data and expected matching evidence.

This work focused on evaluating the quality of AI outputs rather than claiming access to or testing of the internal model architecture.

01

Hallucination checks — reviewing responses for fabricated details, unsupported claims, and conclusions not backed by the supplied evidence.

02

Response accuracy — checking whether AI-generated namescreening assessments aligned with source records and expected outcomes.

03

Reasoning consistency — examining whether explanations were logically consistent with name similarities and matching context.

04

False-positive / false-negative awareness — reviewing potentially misleading hit interpretations and missed matching signals.

05

Evidence-based review — treating LLM output as an aid to investigation, not as the final compliance decision.
